Dhivehi Font is an original Thaana typeface for the Dhivehi language of the Maldives. It covers the complete Unicode Thaana block: the 24 base consonants, naviyani, the 14 Arabic transliteration letters, and all 11 fili.

The letterforms take the calligraphic skeleton of Thaana and steady it for screen use: open counters, even stroke weight, and a flatter posture than the reed-pen tradition it descends from. It is intended for interfaces, government publications and body text, and holds its shape down to 12 px.

Vowel marks are positioned with OpenType anchors rather than fixed offsets, so fili centre correctly on narrow and wide consonants alike.
