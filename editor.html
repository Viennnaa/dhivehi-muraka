Every letter starts blank. Draw the centre-line yourself and the outline is yours. The underlay is a proportion guide: run your line through the middle of the letter, never along its edges. Faruma is there to remind you of the shape, not to be copied — its contours are © 2002 The President's Office.

Tool

Pen: click to drop points along the middle of the stroke. Esc ends the stroke.
Select: drag points or their handles. ⌫ deletes the selected point.
Alt+click a point toggles smooth / corner.
⌘Z undo · ⇧⌘Z redo.
Click a point to select it (turns amber), then use Point width to swell or taper the stroke there.

What the dots mean

 a point on the stroke you're editing
 the one point you selected — Point width acts here
 a point on some other stroke
 a curve handle — drag to bend the line

Points join up by themselves, in the order you click them, within one stroke. Dots that aren't joining are on separate strokes — check the list below. To keep adding to a stroke, click it in that list first, then keep clicking on the canvas.
