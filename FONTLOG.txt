FONTLOG for Dhivehi Muraka

This file provides information on the Dhivehi Muraka Software.

Basic Font Information
----------------------

Dhivehi Muraka is an original Thaana typeface covering the full Unicode Thaana
block (U+0780 to U+07B1): 24 base consonants, naviyani, the 14 Arabic
transliteration letters, and all 11 fili. It also draws the Rufiyaa sign
(U+20C2), the Maldivian currency symbol encoded in Unicode 18.0.

Every outline was drawn by hand from scratch. No existing font was opened,
imported, traced or used as a source of coordinates at any point in the
build. Faruma was displayed on screen as a proportion reference while
drawing, in the way a printed specimen would be; none of its outlines are
present in this font. Each build re-checks every letter against that
reference and refuses any glyph sharing coordinates with it.

ChangeLog
---------

17 September 2026 (Ashrag Rasheed) Dhivehi Muraka v1.001
- Added the Rufiyaa sign (U+20C2), encoded in Unicode 18.0: the letter raa
  with a second parallel bar, following the Maldives Monetary Authority's
  published construction for the symbol. Drawn by hand like every other
  outline here.
- Vertical metrics, and every other glyph, unchanged from v1.000.

7 September 2026 (Ashrag Rasheed) Dhivehi Muraka v1.000
- All 50 Thaana letters drawn from scratch.
- Fili made zero-width and attached with GPOS mark-to-base anchors, so vowel
  marks centre on each consonant rather than sitting at a fixed position.
- GDEF glyph classes; `mark` feature registered under the thaa script.
- 2048 upm, fsType 0, OS/2 v4, USE_TYPO_METRICS, italic angle 0.
